# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

We commit to making participation in Prime Continuim respectful and harassment-free for everyone, regardless of experience, identity, background, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be direct about technical disagreement and kind toward the people involved.
- Critique code, decisions, and evidence—not individuals.
- Assume good intent while still asking for reproducible evidence.
- Respect privacy. Never publish credentials, private paths, transcripts, or personal information.
- Accept maintainer decisions about scope, safety, and release claims.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, sustained disruption, doxxing, or publishing another person's private information are not acceptable in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers through their GitHub profiles. Do not include sensitive information in a public issue. Maintainers may edit or remove content and temporarily or permanently restrict participation when needed to protect the community.

This policy applies in repository spaces and when someone publicly represents the project.
